01 / WHY WE PLAY

FOOTBALL
OPENS DOORS.

A young person's talent deserves a chance to be seen — and the support to grow.

Green City Soccer Academy is an Accra-based football community committed to developing young people through the game. We create a supportive environment where ambition meets opportunity, and where every player's journey matters.

These pages are a starting point for our story. As the academy grows, we'll share more about our people, programmes and impact right here.

01 / OUR MISSION

Develop with purpose.

Help young people in Accra develop football skills, confidence and character through an inclusive, player-first academy experience.

02 / OUR VISION

Opportunity for every talent.

Build a trusted pathway where players can thrive on and off the pitch, and local potential can reach the world stage.

03 / OUR HISTORY

A story still being written.

Green City's history, founding milestones and programme details will be added as the academy's story takes shape.
